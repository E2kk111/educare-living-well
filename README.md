# Educare Living Well™

**A proposed resident-centered program from Absolute Health Group™ and PAC Solution LLC**

Educare Living Well begins by asking residents: **What would help you feel more connected, move with confidence, or enjoy more of your day?**

## Three service pillars

1. **Connection and belonging** — shared-interest activities, music circles, tabletop gardening, optional therapy-dog visits, and one-on-one connection, guided by resident choice.
2. **Balance and bone health** — supervised small-group movement with seated and supported options. Nursing and rehabilitation teams review individual suitability, particularly for osteoporosis, fractures, and fall risk.
3. **Living Well eLMS** — short healthy-aging lessons paired with a resident-chosen action and staff follow-up.

## Resident-centered evaluation

Pilot measures may include self-reported loneliness, confidence joining preferred activities, repeat participation, accessibility requests met, and follow-through on chosen practices. Results should be reviewed with the resident council.

## Funding and clinical boundaries

Illinois Civil Money Penalty (CMP) reinvestment funding is **potential**, subject to current state/federal program rules and approval. No funding award or eligibility is implied.

Educational and activity programming does not replace individualized clinical assessment or rehabilitation. Avoid storing protected health information, resident identifiers, private contact lists, or API keys in this public repository.
