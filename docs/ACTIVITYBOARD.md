# Educare ActivityBoard™

Absolute Health Group™ — Powered by PAC Solution LLC

Enterprise value-based healthcare for post-acute, assisted living and independent living. ActivityBoard is the community interface; ANIKA is the personal interaction layer; Educare delivers education; Outcomes Intelligence measures resident benefit.

## MVP
- Public wall display: activity calendar, locations, accessibility options; no identifiable health or financial information.
- Private resident interface: activity selection, education, goals and optional consent-based assessments.
- Staff dashboard: attendance, education completion, resident goals and aggregate outcomes.
- Funding intelligence: incremental projects, eligibility verification and budget tracking; no guaranteed awards.

## Integration
GitHealth and AION are proposed governance and evidence layers. Lumair and CarePlix are prospective vendors, not established integrations. No clinical inference or biometric collection in the MVP.

## Release gate
Recover complete production source, preserve existing assessment and administrative routes, integrate ActivityBoard as an additive route, verify branding, test accessibility and regression workflows in preview, then promote a tested production build. Do not replace the existing site with a standalone demo.

## Pilot
Proposed 90-day pilot; baseline and follow-up participation, education, resident-selected goals and accessibility. Facility participation and funding unconfirmed.
